Which design direction feels right?

Three approaches for the BrewBeats showcase — all use cinematic premium as the base with the app's warm palette

A

🎬 Cinematic Scrollytelling

Inspiration: Apple product pages, Stripe's website

Full-viewport sections that snap into place. Each section is a "slide" with dramatic entrance animations — text fades up, elements parallax, features reveal on scroll. Dark cinematic backgrounds with warm caramel/gold highlights. Smooth scroll-triggered transitions between sections.

Best for: Walking someone through it in person OR letting them scroll on their own. Maximum "wow" factor.

Presentation mode: Each scroll-snap section = one "slide." You can present it like a deck by scrolling section-by-section.
B

🎵 Music-Reactive Showcase

Inspiration: Spotify Wrapped, Ableton's website

Background has subtle audio-visualizer-style animations (CSS-only, no real audio). Sections flow continuously with parallax depth. Features demonstrated through animated mockups that "play" as you scroll — timeline fills with notes, mixer faders animate, waveforms pulse.

Best for: Showing the product IS music. Every pixel reinforces the theme. Very memorable.

Presentation mode: Continuous scroll with clear section breaks. Animated mockups do the talking.
C

⚡ Hybrid Power Deck

Inspiration: Pitch.com, Linear's landing page

Combines the best of both: snap-scroll "slides" for presentation structure, but with music-reactive animated elements within each slide. Clean typography for readability, cinematic gradients for drama, animated mockups for product credibility. Navigation dots on the side like a slide deck.

Best for: The funding pitch scenario — structured enough to present, impressive enough to explore solo. Professional AND creative.

Presentation mode: Side dots + keyboard arrows for slide navigation. Section counter ("3/10"). Perfect for standing at a screen and clicking through.

💡 My Recommendation: Option C

For a funding pitch, you need structure (so you can present confidently) AND spectacle (so they remember you). Option C gives you slide-deck navigation for presenting, cinematic animations for impact, and the music-reactive elements that make BrewBeats feel alive — all in one file.
